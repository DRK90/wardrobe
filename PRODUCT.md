# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Wardrobe is for people who want a private, technically detailed record of the
clothes they own. They may be planning outfits before work, packing for travel,
checking whether an item needs cleaning, or deciding what to wear based on the
weather. The core context is fast, repeated operational use on a phone, tablet,
or laptop near a closet.

## Product Purpose

Wardrobe is a local-first progressive web app for clothing inventory, outfit
planning, wear history, and optional enrichment through user-configured AI
providers. Success means a user can understand what they own, why an outfit was
recommended, how each item performs in real conditions, and what data stays on
their device. AI and cloud integrations are future work, not current
dependencies.

## Brand Personality

Precise, private, operational.

The interface should feel like a trusted personal database for clothing rather
than a fashion feed. It should support dense inspection, quick capture, and clear
decision records without becoming cold or hostile.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, oversized hero sections, cream-card dashboard
templates, social fashion feeds, decorative outfit mood boards, AI-generated
try-on imagery, glassy panels, gradient text, and any design that hides local
privacy state behind vague branding. Do not make future cloud sync or AI look
like the default path.

## Design Principles

- Treat the wardrobe as an inspectable database, not a gallery.
- Make privacy and storage boundaries visible at the point of action.
- Explain recommendations with item-level evidence, weather, category, and wear
  history.
- Keep the first screen dense but scannable: tables, rails, and inspectors over
  card grids.
- Preserve local speed and offline confidence even when optional providers are
  configured.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Keep keyboard access complete, focus states visible, reduced
motion respected, and contrast high enough for dense tabular use. Do not rely on
color alone for laundry state, weather suitability, or recommendation
confidence.
